Scarlett Automation — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Scarlett Automation is a content publishing and comment management application used to operate the Instagram account @its.scarlett.hayes.

1. Information We Process

The application accesses Instagram account information, published posts, and public comments through Meta's authorized Instagram API.

When managing comments, we may process comment text, comment identifiers, public usernames, post identifiers, and the status of automated responses.

2. How Information Is Used

We use this information to:

  • Schedule and publish Instagram photos and videos.
  • Read comments on our Instagram content.
  • Generate appropriate automated comment replies.
  • Prevent duplicate replies and repeated publications.
  • Maintain operational records and troubleshoot errors.

3. Automated Responses and AI

Some public Instagram comments may be processed by an AI service to generate relevant responses. Comment text may be transmitted to the AI provider for this purpose.

Replies may be automated. Sensitive or inappropriate comments may be skipped or flagged for review.

4. Third-Party Services

Scarlett Automation uses Meta's Instagram API for publishing and comment management.

Cloudinary may be used to host media files for Instagram publishing. Media uploaded for this purpose may be accessible through public URLs.

The application may also use xAI to generate automated comment responses. These services process information under their own applicable privacy terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

Limited comment-processing records and publishing history may be stored in a restricted local database for operational and duplicate-prevention purposes.

We take reasonable steps to protect account credentials and access tokens. We do not intentionally publish private authentication credentials.

6. Data Retention

Operational records may be retained while needed for comment management, duplicate prevention, and troubleshooting. Relevant records may be deleted following a valid deletion request, subject to any applicable legal obligations.

7. Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of information associated with your Instagram comments that is stored by Scarlett Automation.

To request deletion, contact the application operator using the contact email registered for Scarlett Automation in the Meta Developer dashboard.

Please identify your Instagram username and the relevant comment or post. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

We will review the request, verify what records we hold, and delete applicable information where appropriate. Removing locally stored records does not automatically delete content hosted by Instagram.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, or to object to certain processing.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
